# Letter to the principal – Proposal for the school rooftop (about 200 words)

Dear Ms Chan,

I am writing on behalf of the Student Union to propose how the redeveloped rooftop could be put to good use. After collecting opinions from students in every form, we would like to suggest turning the space into a rooftop garden and outdoor study area.

The garden would consist of raised planting beds along the edges, where students could grow vegetables and herbs, with a shaded area of benches and tables in the centre. Solar-powered lights and a small rainwater tank would keep the facility environmentally friendly and inexpensive to maintain.

This facility would benefit students in several ways. First, it would provide a quiet place to revise or read during lunchtime, away from the crowded library and noisy classrooms. Second, caring for plants is proven to reduce stress, which is especially valuable for senior students facing public examinations. Third, the garden could support learning across subjects, as Biology and Geography classes could carry out experiments there, while the vegetables could be used in Home Economics lessons or donated to the needy.

The Student Union would be happy to help manage the garden and organise a planting club. We hope you will consider our proposal.

Yours sincerely,
Chris Wong
Chairperson, Student Union
