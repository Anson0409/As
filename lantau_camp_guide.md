# Lantau Island Adventure Camp – First-Time Visitor Guide

## About Lantau Island Adventure Camp (about 50 words)

Welcome to Lantau Island Adventure Camp! Set among green hills and next to the sea, our camp is the perfect place to escape the city. Whether you love sport, nature or simply relaxing, there is something for everyone. Start at the Visitor Information Centre, where our friendly staff will help you plan your day.

## Top three things to see and do (about 150 words)

**1. Camp under the stars at the Campsite**
Our campsite sits in a quiet, grassy area surrounded by trees. Pitch a tent, light a campfire and enjoy a barbecue with your friends. At night, far from the city lights, you can see hundreds of stars. Remember to bring warm clothes, as it can get chilly after dark.

**2. Get active at the Sports Ground**
If you enjoy sport, head to the Sports Ground. There is a swimming pool, a football pitch and a climbing wall for the more adventurous. Equipment can be borrowed from the Visitor Information Centre for free. Do not forget sunscreen and plenty of water.

**3. Relax at the Beach**
After a busy day, walk down the path to our sandy beach. Rent a kayak, take a swim or simply rest under a palm tree and watch the sunset. Lifeguards are on duty from 9 a.m. to 6 p.m., so please swim only during these hours.

Enjoy your stay!
