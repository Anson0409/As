# Lantau Island Adventure Camp – First-Time Visitor Guide

## About Lantau Island Adventure Camp

Welcome to Lantau Island Adventure Camp! Set among green hills and right next to the sea, our camp is the perfect place to escape the busy city. Whether you love sport, nature or simply relaxing, there is something here for everyone. Start your visit at the Visitor Information Centre, where our friendly staff will gladly help you plan your day.

## Top three things to see and do

The first thing you should not miss is a night at the Campsite. It sits in a quiet, grassy area surrounded by trees, where you can pitch a tent, light a campfire and enjoy a barbecue with your friends. Far from the city lights, the night sky is filled with hundreds of stars, so remember to bring warm clothes because it can get chilly after dark.

Next, if you enjoy being active, head to the Sports Ground. There is a swimming pool, a football pitch and a climbing wall for the more adventurous visitors. Equipment can be borrowed free of charge from the Visitor Information Centre, but do not forget your sunscreen and plenty of water.

Finally, after a busy day, follow the path down to our beautiful sandy Beach. You can rent a kayak, take a swim or simply rest under a palm tree and watch the sunset. Lifeguards are on duty from 9 a.m. to 6 p.m., so please swim only during these hours.

Enjoy your stay!
